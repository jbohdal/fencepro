## 2026-10-28: draft purchase orders show in Pending Orders, marked Draft

- **Jonathan** (Oct 28): "Draft purchase orders from a hand move show in Pending Orders, marked Draft."
- **Where:** Inventory → Sales Orders (the Pending Orders page, PendingOrdersPage.tsx), in a "Draft purchase orders" list above the Sales Orders, and under its own "Drafts" filter. Who opens it is unchanged: owner, admin, manager, office and the Installer (Inventory); the Salesman has no Inventory. The drafts carry costs, so they show only to a login with costs.view; the Installer's phone never receives them (the server leaves BusinessState.purchaseOrders out of the Installer's answers, lib/costRedact.ts BOOKS_KEYS) and cannot change them (billing.change).
- **A draft shows:** the customer, the job's address and fence (style, feet), a purple "Draft" mark, its PO number, the supplier ("Not set": the hand move's draft names none), the day it was made, each line with quantity × unit cost and line total, and the subtotal (integer cents, formatted on screen). Lines are stacked, not a table, so a phone needs no sideways scroll.
- **One "ordered" truth: the Sales Order.** A draft is made from the quote's pull sheet when the job's materials are marked Ordered; the job's Sales Order lists the same parts and also follows approved change orders, and it is what moves the job (lib/materialsFromOrders.ts). So the draft is a working copy, not a second order record (web draftPOs.ts):
  - It names the job's Sales Order (the job's own, else its quote's; never a cancelled one) and says "Same parts as the job's Sales Order (Ordered)" or, part by part, how they differ ("The Sales Order follows change orders; order from it"). "Show the Sales Order" opens it on the same page.
  - **Mark ordered:** marks the Sales Order Ordered when it is still pending (the same step as Mark Ordered on the Sales Order: the job's materials follow) and closes the draft as ordered. A Sales Order already Ordered or Received is left as it is. When the two differ, it asks first and says the Sales Order's parts are the ones kept.
  - **Discard draft:** closes it as cancelled (dated); the job and its Sales Order stay as they are. Not deleted, so a second hand move does not make it again (createDraftPO makes one per job).
  - **Open job:** the job's window in Operations (jobPagePath). Not offered when the job is no longer on the board.
  - A closed draft (ordered or discarded) leaves Pending Orders.
- **Not added, Jonathan's word** (Oct 28 evening): not sendable to the supplier for now ("I order myself"); lines not editable ("The Sales Order is the one list to correct"); drafts not combined by supplier for now. All three are on docs/LATER_IDEAS.md.
- In web draftPOs.ts, purchaseOrderStore.ts closeDraftPO and PendingOrdersPage.tsx; pinned in portal tests/draftPOs.test.ts and draftPOs.db.test.ts.

## 2026-10-28: the Salesman sees only the Salesman's own quotes and customers, plus the ones assigned; the quote list is slim

- **Jonathan** (Oct 28): 1. "Salesman sees only her own quotes and customers, plus any assigned to her." 2. "Yes, do the phone load cut" (a quote's proposal drawing and option copies load when that quote is opened, not with the list).
- **Enforced on the server** (lib/salesScope.ts, mounted in index.ts next to the Installer's crewScoped), for the Salesman's lists and for every record opened by id. Owner, admin, manager and office are unchanged. A record outside the scope opened by id answers 404, never 403 (a 403 would say it exists).
- **The rule.** A customer has no "assigned to" field; what assigns one is a name on a record (the Sales rep on a customer, quote or job, the rep on a pipeline card) or an appointment booked with the Salesman. So:
  - The Salesman's own customers: entered by the Salesman (ownerId), Sales rep is the Salesman, the customer of a pipeline card whose rep is the Salesman, the customer of an appointment booked with the Salesman.
  - Quotes: made by the Salesman (ownerId), Sales rep is the Salesman, and every quote of the Salesman's own customers.
  - A quote assigned to the Salesman brings its customer into the list (name, phone, address, notes), but not that customer's other quotes.
  - Jobs: Sales rep is the Salesman, the job of a quote in scope, every job of the Salesman's own customers. Cards: rep is the Salesman, or the customer is in scope. Appointments: booked with the Salesman, or with a customer in scope.
  - "Is the Salesman": the login's first and last name, or the first name alone, any case or spacing (the same match as the Oct 24 rep backfill). Two logins with the same first name would see each other's first name only records; none do today.
- **Also scoped the same way:** the pipeline board, its pages, search and one card by id (a whole board saved from the Salesman's copy keeps every other card where it was; changing or removing a card outside the scope, or putting a card on a customer outside it, is 404); the follow up badges, a call's answer, a card's next date and history; possible matches; a job's or customer's payments and the receivables list.
- **Website and phone leads** as they come in (nobody assigned yet) are the office's: the Salesman's phone no longer reads /api/intake, and such a customer is not on the Salesman's list until the office assigns it (Sales rep, the card's rep, or an appointment with the Salesman).
- **Same person on file under someone else** (the quote builder's New Customer, customerPick.ts): the Salesman's phone only knows the Salesman's own customers, so it cannot offer "this is them". The server always checks a Salesman's new customer by phone and email (as recordMatches does): any customer on file with them becomes a Possible match on the new customer, for the owner or office to merge with one tap. The Salesman is told only "This phone or email is already on file for another customer; saved as a new customer; the office will merge them if they are the same person", never that customer's name, phone or address; the Salesman's possible match list leaves those matches out, and Merge, Not the same person and Undo merge answer 404 unless both customers are the Salesman's. ## 2026-10-23: matching imported rows to customers on file (every import)

- **Only phone or email find a match:** the same phone (last 10 digits) or the same email (any capitals). A name alone never matches anyone.
- **Same name: attach.** The row goes on the customer on file. Nothing on that customer is overwritten; the import adds its notes, its stored row and its tag.
- **Phone or email match plus a different name: a new customer and a Possible match.** The row becomes a new customer (with its card by the import's own card rule, like any new customer), and its page shows a Possible match notice pointing at the customer on file, so the real ones are merged with one tap (Merge / Not the same person).
- **Several customers match:** the row goes to the single one with the same name. If none has the same name, or more than one does, the row is not imported and is listed for Jonathan.
- **Same name means:** both names cut into words of letters only (accents, capitals, punctuation and spacing ignored), and either all the letters are the same ("John StClair" = "John St. Clair"), or every word of one name, at least two words and each at least two letters, is a word of the other ("Elsa Ipus" = "Roger & Elsa Ipus", "Nonito Palencia Jr." = "Nonito Palencia"). A first name only, a nickname or an initial is a different name: "Ant G" is not "Anthony Gonzalez", "Jennifer D" is not "Jennifer", "Ted miiler" is not "Ted Miller".
- **Undo import** removes the new customers and their Possible matches with everything else the import made.
